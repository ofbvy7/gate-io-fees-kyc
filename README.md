# gate io review: real fees, KYC limits and what the reserve numbers mean for new traders

Most "gate io review" pages answer the wrong question. They list features, paste a screenshot of the trading terminal, and tell you the coin selection is big. True, but useless.

What a reader actually needs to know before depositing money is narrower: what does a round trip cost me, can I get the money out, and is the extra coin coverage worth learning an unfamiliar interface for? Those three answers decide whether this exchange is a fit.

## What Gate is, in two minutes

Gate has been running since 2013 and rebranded to Gate.io in 2017, then shortened the brand to gate.com. It sits among the larger centralised exchanges by volume and has built its reputation on listing assets earlier and more broadly than most competitors — the long tail of altcoins, small caps and new launches that bigger venues skip.

Scale numbers vary by source, so treat round figures with suspicion. Gate's own reserve reporting puts registered users above 55 million, and its mid-2026 snapshot listed daily spot volume above $1.1 billion. Those are self-reported figures, not audited ones.

The coin count is a better illustration of how fuzzy this gets. Gate's marketing copy claims 4,700+ cryptocurrencies. Third-party trackers that count only actively listed pairs show roughly 1,700 coins across about 2,200 pairs. Both numbers can be true at once — a long tail of assets technically supported versus pairs with real liquidity today. If you came for obscure tokens, check the specific ticker you want before signing up rather than trusting the headline number.

Beyond spot, the same account covers perpetual futures, margin, options, CFDs, copy trading, bots, Earn products and Gate Stocks, which carries more than 12,500 US and international equities and ETFs.

## The fee ladder is the whole story

Entry-level spot pricing is **0.10% maker and 0.10% taker**, which is roughly the industry standard and not a reason to pick Gate on its own. Pay the fee in GT, the platform's own token, and it drops to **0.09% per side**.

That "no difference between maker and taker" detail at the bottom of the ladder matters more than it looks. Resting a limit order to add liquidity normally earns you a lower rate. At VIP 0 through VIP 3 on Gate it earns you nothing extra. The split only opens up at VIP 4.

Gate revised the spot and futures schedules on **9 April 2026**. One change in that revision is easy to miss: the 0% spot maker rate was removed from the VIP 10–14 band, so those tiers now pay 0.04% down to 0.008% instead of zero. The fee-free maker rate now starts at the very top of the ladder.

Volume isn't the only door. You can also qualify on **14-day average GT holdings** — VIP 1 opens at 50 GT — or on the total asset value in the account, listed on the fee page as $2,000 for VIP 1 and $100,000 for VIP 10. Hold assets, hold the token, or trade; whichever track you clear first sets your tier, and the system recalculates roughly every six hours.

Two details that change the real cost:

- **Not all volume counts equally.** Per Gate's fee page, spot volume (including Convert) counts at 100%, USDT and BTC perpetuals at 40%, USD1 contracts and options at 20%, and CFD contracts at 10%. A futures trader turning over $100,000 a month is credited with $40,000 of tier progress.
- **The GT discount narrows as you climb.** At VIP 0 it shaves 0.01 percentage points off each side. At the higher tiers the GT column and the standard column converge (VIP 10 shows 0.04% / 0.058% on both).

Run a simple number. At VIP 0, a $10,000 spot round trip costs about **$20** in standard rates, or roughly **$18** paid in GT. At VIP 5 the same round trip drops to around $18.50 standard, and the two-track structure means you'd need $1 million of 30-day volume or about $40,000 of assets sitting on the platform to get there. If that trade size is your normal week, staying at VIP 0 is fine. If it's an hour, the ladder is worth understanding.

> The fee page is the number that actually bills you. Blog posts and comparisons quoting "0.2% spot" are usually recycled from an older table — Gate's current VIP 0 spot rate is 0.1% per side, and the rate shown at order confirmation is authoritative for your account.

## Is Gate safe? The reserve numbers, and their limits

Gate publishes monthly proof-of-reserves snapshots using Merkle tree verification plus zero-knowledge proofs, so you can check whether your own balance was included without publishing your holdings. The snapshot dated **27 July 2026** reports **$7.05 billion in total reserves against $5.98 billion in net customer balances — a 117% ratio**, covering close to 500 asset types, with 26,775 BTC and 456,798 ETH held against user holdings of 21,557 BTC and 374,348 ETH.

Gate has kept the ratio above 100% across recent reports: 115% in June 2024, 128% in May 2025, 122% in March 2026, 115% in June 2026, then 117%. Independent firms validate the process. The company also states it holds a criminal insurance policy and a user protection fund, and third-party reviewers credit it with a broader licensing stack than most exchanges its size — a MiCA authorisation passported across the EEA through a Malta entity, a Japan FSA registration via Gate Japan K.K., plus Cayman CIMA and Italy OAM registrations, and a US FinCEN MSB registration held by the separate Gate.US entity.

Worth repeating what that doesn't mean. None of these is a Tier-1 prudential licence, and the international entity carries **no statutory client compensation scheme**. If it failed, customer funds would go through insolvency proceedings, not a government-backed payout. A monthly snapshot is also a point-in-time check, not a continuous solvency audit.

## What the reviews say — and why the two numbers disagree

Gate's ratings look like two different companies depending on where you look:

- **Trustpilot: around 1.3 out of 5.** Negative reviews cluster around withdrawal delays, sudden account freezes with no explanation, funded accounts that stall at KYC, and support replies that arrive late or read like templates. One user wrote about struggling for hours to withdraw roughly 25 USDT; another, self-described VIP, reported an account locked without notice three days before posting.
- **App stores: roughly 4.3 out of 5**, with an iOS average of 4.26 across more than 5,000 ratings and Google Play in a similar range. Praise focuses on the trading terminal, chart layout and early access to new tokens.

A third-party analysis of review patterns found something more useful than an average: around **70% of the lowest-rated reviews** centre on withdrawal friction, account freezes, weak support, or disputes over derivative liquidations, while most of the 4–5 star reviews come from people whose trades and withdrawals simply worked. That's not the profile of a broken platform or a scam. It is the profile of a large exchange where the happy majority never contact support and the unhappy minority are stuck in queue, often over exactly the two things that matter most — money leaving the account, and someone replying.

Two recurring complaint themes deserve specifics rather than adjectives. Token delistings can leave holders with a converted remainder that's far smaller than expected, and leveraged ETF-style tokens have been consolidated or terminated with notice that users say they never received. Check the announcement feed for anything you hold long-term.

## Who Gate suits, and who should look elsewhere

**Reasonable fit if you:**

- Trade altcoins, small caps, or new listings and want access before they reach larger venues
- Want spot, futures, options, stocks, CFDs and Earn products behind one KYC check
- Already use TradingView-style interfaces and care more about listing depth than a minimalist UI
- Want ongoing reserve disclosure you can verify against your own account

**Look elsewhere if you:**

- Want a 0% maker rate from day one — Gate's entry tiers charge identical maker and taker fees
- Require a Tier-1 licence or a statutory compensation scheme behind your deposit
- Are a beginner who wants a two-button interface; the multi-level menu takes real getting used to
- Live somewhere the global platform doesn't serve

On availability: the global platform does not accept US or UK retail customers, US residents are routed to the separate Gate.US entity with a far smaller listing set, and Japan is served through the locally registered entity. Rules change often, so confirm your country's status during registration rather than relying on any review, this one included.

## Signing up: KYC, vouchers and the part everyone gets wrong

Registration takes a few minutes with an email or phone number. Full identity verification is then required — typically before your first deposit, not just before withdrawal, which is stricter than many exchanges. Have your ID and a face capture ready; the verification step is where a large share of the negative reviews start, particularly for users in countries where the entity's coverage is ambiguous.

[👉 Create your Gate account and start at VIP 0](https://bit.ly/GateVIP)

The rewards centre advertises a headline figure of **10,000+ USDT** in new-user rewards. Read the mechanics before you count on it:

- The amount is a **maximum across staged tasks**, mostly in the advanced trading track, which requires net deposits and cumulative volume far beyond a typical first month.
- Rewards are issued as **vouchers, not withdrawable cash**. Futures vouchers can fund a position and you keep the profit; fee-rebate vouchers offset trading charges. Neither is cash in your balance.
- Beginner tasks — registration, identity verification, a first deposit (often ≥50 USDT), a first spot or futures trade, app download — come with **deadlines, commonly 15 days** from signup, and each reward must be **claimed manually** in the rewards hub before its voucher expires.
- Sub-accounts, API-only accounts and affiliate accounts are excluded, and stablecoin-to-stablecoin trades generally don't count toward reward volume.

The referral rebate is separate from all of that and doesn't expire with the campaigns: registering through a referral link attaches a code to your account, and a share of your trading fees comes back as rebate. What's easy to get wrong — the code normally **can't be added after registration**, so if you're going to use one, it has to be in the field when the account is created, before the welcome tasks start.

## The verdict

Gate earns its place on two things: listing breadth and published reserves. Its spot entry fees are ordinary, its maker rates stay flat until VIP 4, and the payment is a UI built for people who already know what they're doing.

Judge it by how you'd use it. As a long-tail altcoin venue with verifiable reserves and one account for spot, futures and stocks, it's a defensible pick for traders who accept that the international entity offers no compensation scheme. As a cheap first exchange for a cautious beginner who wants a 0% maker rate and a hand-held onboarding, the pitch is weaker — and the withdrawal complaints in the review pile are worth reading before you deposit anything you can't afford to leave sitting.

### FAQ

**Is Gate.io legit or a scam?**
It's a legitimate, long-running exchange: operating since 2013, MiCA-authorised in the EEA, FSA-registered in Japan, publishing monthly proof-of-reserve snapshots. Trustpilot sits around 1.3/5 while app stores sit above 4.3/5, and the negative reviews mostly concern withdrawal delays, account freezes and slow support rather than an inability to withdraw in general.

**What are Gate's spot trading fees?**
0.100% maker and taker at VIP 0, or 0.09% per side when fees are paid in GT. Rates fall across 17 VIP tiers, decided by 30-day trading volume, 14-day average GT holdings, or account asset value — whichever threshold you clear first.

**Are futures cheaper than spot on Gate?**
Yes. USDT-margined perpetuals start at 0.020% maker and 0.050% taker, versus 0.100% per side on spot at the entry tier. Funding is charged or paid every 8 hours on top of that.

**Does Gate require KYC?**
Yes. Full identity verification including a face check is required, and in practice it's needed before your first deposit. Verification deadlines matter for the new-user rewards — commonly 15 days from registration.

**Can I use Gate in the US or UK?**
The global platform doesn't accept US or UK retail customers. US residents are routed to the separate Gate.US entity, which lists far fewer assets. Availability changes frequently, so verify your country during signup.
